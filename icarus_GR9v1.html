Icarus (Recel, 1977)

Quick strategy synopsis

Shoot the left drop targets to advance the bonus and light goodies such as extra ball and double bonus at the correspondingly numbered captive ball drop targets on the right. Be sure to collect those goodies before completing the left bank, because completing the left bank resets both drop target banks and unlights the goodies. Each left drop target down also lights one top standup target or top lane for a bonus advance. Building and doubling the bonus is the source of the overwhelming majority of points on Icarus.

Shots and table features

Left drop targets, and everything they affect

The left drop targets are labelled 1-2-3-4-5. Each target down scores 5,000 points and a bonus advance, and lights other features around the table.
The 1-2-3 standup targets at the top of the game and the 4-5 rollover lanes in the top right score 500 points, or 5,000 and a bonus advance when lit. Each of these is lit when the corresponding left drop target is down. The rightmost top lane to the right of the 5 lane always scores 1,000 points and 1 bonus advance.
The drop targets in the captive ball area score 5,000 points and a bonus advance each when not lit. The three targets in this bank are individually lit when #1, #3, or #5 in the left bank are down, respectively. The left captive ball drop target awards Extra Ball when lit; the center captive ball drop target awards 50,000 points and a bonus advance when lit; the right captive ball drop target awards Double Bonus when lit. The standup targets behind the captive ball drop targets always score 500 points only. Completing the captive ball drop targets only resets that bank. Completing the 1-2-3-4-5 left drop targets resets both banks, which also unlights all features corresponding to the numbered drop targets.

Miscellaneous features

Bumpers always score 100 points.

There are two side lanes on the right along the edge of the table that both award 1,000 points and a bonus advance.

There is a gap in the wooden wall that forms the right side of the playfield, wide enough for the ball to reenter the shooter lane for a replunge at any time. There is no gate feature or scoring associated with this. The gap back to the shooter lane cannot be shot directly, and is most often accessed off of the left slingshot.

Bottom of the table

Icarus has a conventional in/out lane setup. Slingshots score 100 points. In lanes score 500 points. Out lanes score 10,000 points. There is no center post, kickback, or free ball gate.

The left out lane can be lit for Special, but I have never seen this occur. It does not seem to be tied to completing any particular bank(s) of drop targets or reaching any particular values of bonus. My theory, which is not confirmed, is that the Special is lit when all three captive ball drop targets are hit at once, which requires knocking down the 1, 3, and 5 targets in the left bank while leaving 2 and/or 4 standing so that the drop target banks do not reset.

Bonus and bonus multiplier

Bonus is advanced by any drop target, lit 1-2-3 standup targets, lit 4-5 top lanes, and any of the three rollover lanes along the right side of the table. If the game is on easy settings, anything that awards 5,000 and a bonus advance (any drop, lit 1-2-3 targets, or lit 4-5 lanes) will give 2 bonus advances instead. Double bonus can be earned on any ball by knocking down drop target 5 on the left, then knocking down the rightmost captive ball drop target. Max bonus is 2x 150,000 = 300,000 points. There is no mid-ball bonus collect or bonus holdover of any kind. The first bonus advance is not given for free, so it is possible to drain with 0 bonus.
