Attack (Playmatic, 1980)

Not to be confused with Attack from Mars (Bally Williams, 1995).

Quick strategy synopsis

Open the right out lane gate whenever possible by shooting the captive ball. The gate closes when used or when making the top center saucer. With the gate open, shoot back to the top of the table for A-B-C-D letters; if you have most or all of them, hit the lone yellow drop targets in the top left and lower sides for bonus multipliers. The left saucer behind the spinner awards an increasing value of up to 40,000 points, but reduces the bonus multiplier by one step if you have one, so the right orbit is the preferred way to get the ball back up the game.

Shots and table features

A-B-C-D-E-F letters

A and D are star rollovers at the top of the table. B and C are lanes at the top of the table. E and F are the two right in lanes. The A and D star rollovers always score 5,000 points. Collecting A lights the B and F lanes, and collecting D lights the C and E lanes, The B, C, E, and F lanes score 500 points when not lit, or 10,000 when lit. Each of B, C, E, and F lights a star rollover in the left in lane when collected; these star rollovers score 1,000 points or 10,000 when lit.

Top saucer, spinner, and captive ball

The left spinner scores 100 points per spin and rotates the value of the Extra Bonus, indicated by red lights going up the playfield. The Extra Bonus can be worth 10,000, 20,000, 30,000, 40,000, 50,000, 60,000, or 60,000 plus light extra ball. Making the top center saucer scores the current Extra Bonus, resets the Extra Bonus to 10,000, closes the right out lane gate if it is open, and lights the captive ball on the right to score 30,000 points on the next hit.

The captive ball has a rollover switch and a target at the end of its lane. The rollover switch always scores 1,000 points. The target at the end scores 1,000 if it is completely unlit, but it can be lit to score 30,000 by making the top saucer or Special by having all three drop targets be down at the same time. Collecting a 30,000 or Special from the captive ball unlights that award.

Left saucer and drop targets

There are 3 lone yellow drop targets around the game: one in the upper left, one in the lower left, and one in the lower right. Each drop target down scores 7,000 points and increases the bonus multiplier in the sequence 2x-3x-5x. If both of the lower drop targets are down, the star rollover in the right orbit scores 15,000 points instead of 500. If all 3 drop targets are down, the captive ball is lit for Special.

The upper left saucer behind the spinner starts each ball completely unlit, meaning it scores 1,000 points. Shots to the saucer increase its value to 8,000, then 15,000, then 40,000 points, before going back to 1,000 and repeating the cycle. Each shot to the saucer also raises one drop target around the playfield, which decreases the bonus multiplier and can unlight the right orbit 15,000 or the captive ball Special. Re-raised drop targets can be knocked down once again to re-score the 7,000 points and requalify any associated features.

Pop bumpers

Bumpers score 100 points, or 1,000 when lit. Exactly one bumper is lit at any one time, and they alternate each time either bumper is hit.

Bottom of the table

On the left, there is one in lane and one out lane, and they are about double the height of normal in/out lanes. The left out lane scores 10,000 points or Special when lit, and is lit by collecting all of A-B-C-D-E-F in one ball. Scoring in this out lane is located at a star rollover near the apron which the ball is not forced to press; you may need to nudge the ball off of a post in the bottom left of the in lane to earn this scoring. In any case, it is fairly rare for the ball to end up in the left out lane at all due to the size of the openings. The left in lane has 4 star rollovers, which score 1,000 or 10,000 when lit, and are lit from top to bottom by collecting the letters B, C, E, and F respectively. The plastic that forms the edge of the left out lane covers up two gaps that allow the ball to enter the left out lane at places other than the standard top entrance.
On the right, there are two in lanes, which award the letters E and F. They score 500 points or 10,000 when lit and are lit by collecting D and A respectively. The right out lane scores 15,000 points, and can be lit for extra ball by collecting Extra Ball from Extra Bonus at the top saucer. There is a gate in the right out lane that redirects the ball back to the shooter lane for a replunge; it is opened by hitting the captive ball, and closed by making the top saucer or using the gate itself. The rollover switch is located above the gate, making it possible to collect an extra ball without losing your current ball if the gate is open.

Bonus and bonus multiplier

Bonus multiplier is equal to 2x, 3x, or 5x depending on whether there are 1, 2, or 3 yellow drop targets down. The top left saucer behind the spinner re-raises one drop target, therefore removing an earned bonus multiplier, though that multiplier can be recollected by knocking down the drop target again. End of ball bonus is calculated as 10,000 points times multiplier for each A-B-C-D-E-F letter collected on that ball, for a maximum of 300,000 points in bonus. This can be very significant, and is why you may not want to shoot the spinner lane as a way to get back to the top of the table, since doing so decreases your bonus multiplier if you land in the upper left saucer. There is no way to collect any part of the bonus mid-ball or carry any part of the bonus to future balls. The value indicated by Extra Bonus is not part of the end of ball bonus and can only be collected at the top saucer while a ball is in play.

Tilt ends the ball in play only. Power is not cut to the flippers when the game is tilted, but the bumpers will not pop and none of the game's features will give any points until the ball is allowed to drain.
