Fiesta / Fandango (Playmatic, 1976)

Fiesta is the 4-player version. Fandango is a 1-player version with identical rules and scoring.

Quick strategy synopsis

Hit an unlit A-B-C-D target to light it and unlight the corresponding lane in the left waterfall, or vice versa. Having all 4 lanes or all 4 targets lit at once qualifies double bonus at one of the left standup targets. With double bonus earned, shoot the top right saucer for triple bonus. Build bonus with left standup targets or the top mushroom bumper. After bonus is built up and multiplied, shoot the left waterfall lanes for points by the 10,000s or shoot the top right saucer for 30,000 per shot.

Shots and table features

On the plunge

At the top of the table are one lane and one rollover button. The top lane scores 30,000 points, and is also lit for Special if all of the A-B-C-D waterfall lanes are lit. The rollover button scores 500 points and advances the bonus. It is very difficult to reach either of these anytime after the initial plunge on a ball.

A-B-C-D sequence, bonus, and bonus multipliers

Whether they are lit or not, the A-B-C-D targets on the right score 1,000 points, and the A-B-C-D rollover lanes on the left score 10,000. For each letter, either the lane or the target will be lit, but not both. Hitting an unlit target or rollover lane will light it, but unlight the corresponding switch with the same letter. Having all of the A-B-C-D targets lit at the same time lights the right out lane for Special and lights the middle left standup target for Double Bonus. Having all of the A-B-C-D lanes lit at the same time lights the top lane for Special, lights the left out lane for Extra Ball, and lights the lower left standup target for Double Bonus. Disrupting the complete set of A-B-C-D unlights the Special lanes and Double Bonus targets. The status of the A-B-C-D lights is preserved from ball to ball, unless the ball drains with the set complete on one side, in which case the game will break up the set by toggling one letter (usually D).

When Double Bonus has been collected, the top right saucer will upgrade it to Triple Bonus in addition to giving its usual score of 30,000 points.

Base bonus is advanced by the top rollover button, the mushroom bumper in the upper left, or either left standup target. Each bonus advance is worth 10,000 points in base bonus instead of the usual 1,000. The first bonus advance may or may not be given for free, seemingly dependent on an operator setting: it may or may not be possible to drain with 0 bonus. Max bonus is 3x 90,000 = 270,000 points. There is no mid-ball bonus collect or bonus holdover of any kind.

Bumper lights

When the bumpers are lit, they scores 1,000 points per hit, and the left in lane scores 500 points. When the bumpers are not lit, they score only 100 points per hit, but the left in lane is lit for 30,000 points. The status of the bumpers (on or off) is preserved from ball to ball; rollover buttons in the center of the playfield turn the bumpers on (left) or off (right).

Accessing the waterfall

The waterfall lane structure on the right has two entrances in the top right of the table, with an Advance Bonus mushroom bumper in between. The left entrance curves quickly, goes down the B and D lanes, and should drop the ball cleanly into the left in lane. The right entrance curves more broadly, going down the A and C lanes before a post pops the ball toward the center of the playfield. It's difficult to drain down the left out lane after a waterfall shot (or, indeed, at all) barring an unlucky bounce or an ill-advised nudge.

Bottom of the table

The left side of the table bottom is conventional, with one in lane, one out lane, and one slingshot. The in lane scores 500 points or 30,000 when lit, and is lit whenever the pop bumpers are not lit. The out lane scores 10,000 points and is lit for Extra Ball if all of the A-B-C-D lanes are lit. On the right, there is no in lane and the flipper backs up directly to the slingshot. The right out lane scores 10,000 points and is lit for Special if the A-B-C-D targets are all lit. The right out lane entrance is not vertical, and bends back to the left toward the center of the playfield. Slingshots score 10 points.

To my knowledge, extra balls and Specials cannot be set to points. Maximum 1 extra ball per ball in play. Tilt ends the ball in play only.
