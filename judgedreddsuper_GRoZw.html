Judge Dredd Super Game (Bally Williams, 1993)

This page contains only information specific to Super Game. For a full rulesheet of the standard game, click here.

About Super Game

Super Game is a feature exclusive to Judge Dredd pinball. By adding 2 credits to the game instead of 1, you can press the green start button on the front of the game to play Super Game. In a multiplayer setting, each player that wants to play Super Game must press the green start button and pay the 2 credits; the yellow start button will just start a normal game for that player, and it is possible to mix and match normal and Super games within a single multiplayer game. If playing Judge Dredd in a tournament, pay very close attention to which version of the game you are told to play and which start button you press.

Rules

In a Super Game, every ball starts with a 2-ball multiball with 10 seconds of unlimited ball save. There are 4 different Super Game multiballs; the first one played seems to be random, but after that they go in the order listed below. Each Super Game has its own jackpot rules, but there are 2 features that apply to more than one Super Game multiball:

  • Spelling Judge at the center drop targets before returning to single-ball play upgrades the 2-ball multiball into a 6-ball Super Multiball. This does not affect the scoring of that multiball, and there is very minimal additional ball save given when a multiball is upgraded. This applies to all 4 Super Game multiballs.
  • Hitting the Advance Crime Scene targets near the upper right flipper progresses you through Warning, Misdemeanor, Felony, and Class X Felony. Reaching Class X Felony with 4 shots to these targets lights a shot somewhere around the game for 100,000,000 points for 10 seconds. Which shot is lit depends on which Super Game multiball is being played, and will be addressed below. Once the 100,000,000 either times out or is collected, this feature is gone, with no way to relight it during a single Super Game multiball. This applies to all Super Game multiballs except Destroy Deadworld.

The four Super Game multiballs are as follows:

  • Prison Break: the left orbit, Sniper Tower popper in the top right, and right ramp are lit red. Make a lit shot to score 7,500,000 points and unlight it. Making all 3 scores a Super Jackpot worth 52,500,000 and relights all three shots. Repeat until there is just 1 ball left in play. After hitting Advance Crime Scene 4 times, the game will display Shoot Subway for Hundred Million, but the 100,000,000 shot is actually the left ramp.
  • Mad Bomber: shoot the left or right ramp to qualify the Super Jackpot at the Sniper Tower popper at a value of 15,000,000. Further shots to the left or right ramp add 7,500,000 to the Super Jackpot, to a maximum of 52,500,000. Collecting the Super Jackpot resets its value and forces it to be requalified at a ramp. After hitting Advance Crime Scene 4 times, the game will display Shoot Subway for Hundred Million, and it means it this time (the Subway is the saucer behind the Judge center drop targets).
  • Traffic Jam: jackpots are always available at the Subway saucer behind the Judge drops. Making the saucer scores a jackpot worth 5,000,000 points times the number of Judge targets currently knocked down, then resets the drops. Lit Judge letters are not reset, so there's no harm or benefit to collecting a jackpot before upgrading the multiball. After hitting Advance Crime Scene 4 times, the 100,000,000 shot is at the Sniper Tower popper.
  • Destroy Deadworld: every third shot to the left ramp destroys Deadworld, scoring a super jackpot worth 45,000,000 the first time and 52,500,000 all subsequent times. This Super Game multiball does not seem to have a 100,000,000 shot like the other three do; the Advance Crime Scene target is lit, but the game will never actually progress through the four crime levels.

After a Super Game multiball ends, you return to the normal Judge Dredd game rules with your single ball. Super Game multiballs and the normal game rules are completely detached; you cannot start Chain Link modes or play the normal multiball during a Super Game round, and you cannot restart a Super Game round for the rest of the ball once you've transitioned back to normal rules. Using a buy-in ball on a Super Game costs the usual 1 credit, not 2. Super Game high scores have their own leaderboard, with the factory default high score for Super Game being double that of the normal game.
