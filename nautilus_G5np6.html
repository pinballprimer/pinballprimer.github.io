Nautilus (Zaccaria, 1977)

This game's layout and scoring rules are extremely similar to the 4-player Combat (Zaccaria, 1977).

Quick strategy synopsis

Lit rollover buttons at the top of the playfield score 50,000 points and add 50,000 to the "Poulp Bonus" at the end of the ball. The upper right kicker lane awards double bonus, making the bonus worth up to 1,000,000 points. Making either the top left saucer or upper right kicker flashes the bumpers for 10,000 per pop and lights the spinner for 10,000 per spin for a few seconds. Combine these features to shoot to the top of the table with a purpose, earning max double bonus early and often, and pressing lit top buttons as much as possible.

Shots and table features

Top buttons

There are 10 rollover buttons at the top of the game. At any one time, 5 of the 10 are lit. Unlit buttons score 100 points. Lit buttons score 50,000 points and a bonus advance. Which buttons are lit alternates anytime exactly 1,000 points are scored anywhere in the game.

Upper left saucer

The upper left saucer begins the game at a score of 500 points. One hole advance is scored by making either in lane or registering about 5-10 spins of the spinner. After 3 advances, the upper left saucer value increases to 5,000 points. After 6 total advances, the value increases to 50,000 points. After 9 total advances, the upper left saucer value is maxed out at 400,000 points plus an extra ball. The 500, 5,000, and 50,000 values can be collected repeatedly, but earning the 400,000 causes the hole to reset to 0 advances and a value of 500 points. The hole value does not reset between balls; only at the beginning of a game or after collecting the 400,000. Making the upper left saucer also flashes the bumpers and lights the spinner for about 15 seconds.

Upper right kicker lane

Scores 50,000 points and lights Double Poulp Score, which is a 2x bonus multiplier. This is very difficult (if not impossible) to shoot directly, and usually requires the assistance of the upper or lower right pop bumpers.

Flash Score feature (pop bumpers and spinner)

In normal play, pop bumpers are unlit and score 1,000 points each, and the unlit spinner also scores 1,000 points per spin. For about 15 seconds after making either the upper left saucer or the upper right kicker lane, the bumpers will flash and score 10,000 points each, and the spinner will be lit for 10,000 per spin. This timer cannot be ended prematurely, so if the ball drains with the spinner lit and bumpers flashing, these features can be briefly carried into the next ball if the bonus counts quickly enough.

Drop targets

The eight drop targets on the right of the game spell Nautilus. At the start of the game, each target down scores 1,000 points. Knocking down all 8 targets resets them and increases their value to 10,000 points each. Completing the drop targets a second time increases their value to 100,000 points each and lights the red standup target near the lower right pop bumper for a Special, which scores a free game or an extra ball. While 100,000-point drop targets is tempting, Nautilus is often a fast-paced game, and the long term investment of the drop targets usually does not pay out as well as focusing on lit top rollover buttons, bonus advances, and potentially the upper left saucer if the value is high. Also, the location of posts that hold up the plastic artwork above the drop targets make it so that the T and I targets in the middle of the 8-bank can be quite stubborn and may not drop down when hit.

Bottom of the table

Nautlius has a nearly conventional in/out lane setup. However, part of the rail separating the in and out lanes is cut off. A ball rolling up a flipper quickly can roll through this gap and fall out, but a ball going down and out lane can be nudged off a small pin in the out lane and knocked back into play. In lanes score 10,000 points and one hole advance. Out lanes score 10,000 points.

Bonus and bonus multiplier

The end of ball bonus, or "Poulp Score", can only be advanced by pressing lit top rollover buttons. Each advance is worth 50,000 points in base bonus. The first bonus advance is never given for free, so it is possible to drain with 0 bonus. Bonus can be doubled by making the upper right kicker lane at any time; the double bonus is also never given for free. Max bonus is 2x 500,000 = 1,000,000 points, which is an extremely important component of scoring on Nautilus. Building and doubling the bonus should be the top priority on any ball.

Maximum 1 extra ball per ball in play. I have not confirmed the tilt penalty, but it is likely just the ball in play rather than the whole game.
