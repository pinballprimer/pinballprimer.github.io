Black Magic / Black Magic 4 (Recel, 1980)

Black Magic is a 1-player version, while Black Magic 4 is a 4-player version which seems to be much more common. As far as I can tell, they have identical layouts, rules, and scoring.
This game has significant layout and rules similarities with Mr. Doom/Mr. Evil (Recel, 1978/79).

Quick strategy synopsis

Each drop target scores 5,000 points and advances the bonus. Completing drop target banks lights neat stuff around the table, including 50,000 points and both captive balls after 1 drop completion and double bonus at the right captive ball after 2 drop completions. Max the bonus out at 150,000 points at the drop targets, then shoot captive balls for 50,000 each until the cows come home. Avoid trying to catch the ball on the left flipper by simply holding it up due to the cut off in/out lane on the left.

Shots and table features

Top lanes area

There are a total of 6 lanes at the top of the table.
The leftmost top lane scores 5,000 points and is lit for Double Bonus after one drop target completion.
The second top lane from left scores 50,000 points, and is the ideal outcome of any plunge.
The third top lane from left scores 5,000 points and is lit for Extra Ball after one drop target completion.
To the right of the three top lanes is a spinner which always scores 10 points per spin. It's pretty hard for the ball to come down the spinner lane off the plunge, but this lane is how you get back to the top of the table to collect lit goodies after completing some drop targets.
Far to the right, detached from the other top lanes, are two more lanes that always score 500 points. This rightmost lane will usually have the ball come down the right side of the table, past a standup target (which scores 500) and down the right side lane (which scores 5,000) which feeds the ball toward the right flipper.

Pop bumpers, of which there are two at the top of the table, always score 100.

Drop targets and captive balls

At the start of any ball, captive balls are unlit and score 5,000 points. Each drop target down in either bank scores 5,000 points and a bonus advance. Completing a bank of drop targets resets both banks.
The first time either drop target bank is completed: both captive balls will be lit for 50,000 points, the leftmost top lane will be lit for Double Bonus, and the third top lane from left will be lit for Extra Ball.
The second time either drop target bank is completed: the left captive ball will light for Extra Ball and the right captive ball will light for Double Bonus.
The third time either drop target bank is completed: the right out lane will light for Special.

Use drop targets to build the base bonus, then use captive balls to double the bonus and score as many repeatable 50,000s as possible. If extra ball and Special can be set to a point value, I do not know what that value is.

There appears to be a setting allowing each individual drop target to award 2 bonus advances instead of 1.

Bottom of the table

Both sides of the table bottom include one in lane, one out lane, and one slingshot. Above the out lane are additional rollover lanes; on both sides, these score 10,000 points. The left in and out lane score 10,000 and 20,000 points respectively. The rail between the left in and out lanes is cut off, meaning a left out lane ball can be nudged into the in lane off of yet another pin in the lower left of the game, but a ball rolling up the left flipper can also fall into the out lane as well. The right "upper out lane" curves the ball to the left toward the joint in/out lane entrance; the right in lane does not start vertical, and slants outwards to near the out lane entrance. The left "upper out lane" has a pin below it which can be used to nudge the ball back into play. The right out lane scores 5,000 points, plus Special after 3 drop target completions. The right in lane scores 500 points only.

Bonus and bonus multiplier

Each bonus advance increases the base bonus by 10,000 instead of the usual 1,000. Bonus advances can only be earned by shooting drop targets. The first bonus advance is not given for free, and it is possible to drain with 0 bonus. Drop targets may give 2 bonus advances instead of 1. Double Bonus is earned by making the leftmost top lane after completing 1 bank of drop targets, or by shooting the right captive ball after completing 2 banks of drop targets. I believe max bonus is 2x 150,000 = 300,000 points. Bonus is a very significant portion of scoring, so take care not to tilt. There is no mid-ball bonus collect, no way to hold the base bonus or multiplier between balls, and no bonus multiplier past 2x.

Reading the score displays

This game, as with some other solid state games from this era of Spanish pinball, uses score displays with a lot of unlabeled dots, which mean the following:

  • A dot in the upper right corner of a display indicates that that player is part of the current game. (e.g. in a 2 player game on Mr. Doom, only the upper 2 displays will have this dot.)
  • A flashing dot in the lower right corner indicates which player's turn it is.
  • A dot to the left of all 6 digits indicates a rolled score of 1,000,000 points. I do not believe there is any way for the game to indicate scores above 2,000,000.
  • Dots near the bottom of every digit in a display indicates that that display is currently showing the high score to date on the machine.
