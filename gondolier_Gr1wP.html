Gondolier (Gottlieb, 1958) / Serenade (Alben, 1959)

Gondolier and Serenade are the same game with different artwork.

Game rules

The top rollover button, the 4 middle side lanes, and the 2 out lanes spin the Target Value in the center of the playfield. Hitting a standup target or gobble hole scores 10 times the target value, or 100 times the target value when lit. Standup targets and rollover lanes move which target or gobble hole is lit. One target is always lit; there will only be a gobble hole lit if the bumpers are also lit. The top rollover button scores 5 points; rollover lanes score 5 points or 50 when lit, and alternate being lit or not based on the match unit, which is moved by hitting any switch around the playfield.

Pop bumpers score 1 point or 10 when lit. At the top of the game are 2 pairs of rollunder gates; the left gate in each pair turns the bumpers off, and the right gate in each pair turns the bumpers on. Either the blue (upper) or green (lower) bumpers will be lit at a time, never all 4 at once. Switch hits around the playfield alternate whether the blue or green bumpers are lit.

Slingshots score 1 point or 10 when lit and also alternate based on switch hits. There is no extra ball feature or end of ball bonus. Tilt ends game, but only for the player who tilted.
